## Initial offer

Sell a standalone local-first application or commercial-use rights with a one-time purchase. Keep task ownership, export, recovery, and offline access independent of any purchase server. Start with explicit order-based delivery; do not add an account service or embed payment/signing secrets in the static application.

The verified static distribution is built with `python3 tools/build_release.py`. The archive contains runtime files and integrity metadata, not development caches or personal task data. Distribution generation does not publish a release, accept money, or grant rights.

Before listing it for sale, decide and publish:

- The eligible buyer, permitted personal/business use, device or seat scope, and redistribution limits.
- The one-time price, currency, taxes, payment processor, invoicing and refund process.
- Which version is licensed, the update entitlement period, and the scope of ongoing security fixes and support.
- A working private support contact, applicable privacy and purchase terms, and the recovery procedure.

An actual commercial order must identify the licensed party, rights, scope, version, entitlement and evidence of purchase. A generic public file, client-side boolean or archive checksum is not proof of purchase. Do not retrofit paid checks that block existing users from exporting their data.

## Future professional features

CSV reporting and JSON restoration are now part of the available core. Validate demand for richer recurring-task workflows, advanced saved views, and large-workspace tools before implementing a paid boundary. Keep the currently available core usable. Cloud sync, collaboration, and subscription pricing need a separate product and operating-cost decision.

## Launch evidence

Require the automated quality and browser checks, a verified static artifact, real HTTPS/update/rollback checks, realistic workspace performance, mobile/keyboard checks, and a tested restoration path. Record actual browser/OS versions. CI configuration alone is not evidence that a browser passed. See [the roadmap](product-roadmap.md) and release notes for current evidence and missing checks.
